Food safety rules

Share

Freshly cooked food, covered, at a safe temperature, in clean containers, collected before the deadline.

Never share

Expired, contaminated or spoiled food, plate leftovers, or anything not stored at the right temperature.

Follow FSSAI guidance

Real-world food recovery must follow applicable FSSAI rules. NGOs are verified by an admin before they can request food.
